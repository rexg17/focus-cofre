## 0.1.0 — first release · primeira versão · primera versión

**English**
- Coach: steps, next action with explanation, monthly plan, financial health, check-in, cash forecast, "what if…?".
- Work (projects, hourly rate, AI tools, MEI), content channel, personal, cash, statement, invest.
- Several currencies (€, R$, US$) with FX per transaction; extra pay; estimated account balances.
